\section{Project Overview \& Core Idea}

\subsection{The Elevator Pitch}
\textbf{TripMate AI} is an intelligent, real-time collaborative web application engineered to eliminate the friction, indecision, and organizational fatigue inherent in group decision-making. Whether coordinating multi-day vacations, finding the perfect dinner spot, organizing a cafe hangout, or planning entertainment outings, TripMate AI transforms chaotic discussions into an actionable, consensus-driven plan in \textbf{under 15 minutes}.

\subsection{The Core Paradigm: Democratic Human-in-the-Loop AI}
Unlike standard AI chatbots that generate disconnected text responses, TripMate AI integrates an autonomous \textbf{Multi-Agent AI System (LangGraph Python)} directly into a structured collaborative planning pipeline:
\begin{center}
    \textbf{1. Event Creation} $\longrightarrow$ \textbf{2. AI/Manual Proposal} $\longrightarrow$ \textbf{3. Democratic Voting} $\longrightarrow$ \textbf{4. Owner Confirmation} $\longrightarrow$ \textbf{5. Debt Settlement}
\end{center}
The platform operates on a strict \textbf{Human-in-the-Loop} principle: the AI acts as an expert research assistant producing structured, modifiable \texttt{DRAFT} plans, while humans retain complete democratic authority through voting, granular modifications, and owner sign-off.

\subsection{Key Value Propositions}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Autonomous Multi-Agent Intelligence:} Specialized sub-agents automatically search live locations via Google Places, extract verified restaurant menus and activity pricing, compute optimal route sequences, and balance budgets.
    \item \textbf{DeepSeek-V4 Next-Gen Reasoning:} Integrates the latest \textbf{DeepSeek-V4} model family, utilizing high-throughput tool calling for data retrieval and deep reasoning tokens to resolve split-vote deadlocks among members.
    \item \textbf{Automated 3-Table Financial Settlement:} Eliminates post-trip financial headaches with an algorithm that tracks pool advances vs. actual spending and computes the minimum peer-to-peer bank transfers needed.
\end{itemize}

\section{Problem Statement \& Motivation}

Group planning among 3 to 10 participants consistently breaks down across four major pain points:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{\arabic*.}]
    \item \textbf{Endless Debates \& Information Fragmentation:} Casual questions like \textit{''Where are we eating tonight?''} spawn hundreds of unstructured messages across messaging apps (Zalo, Messenger), causing crucial details to get buried.
    \item \textbf{Single Organizer Overload:} One person typically bears the entire burden of reading reviews, checking opening hours, comparing hotels, estimating transit times, and individually polling each friend.
    \item \textbf{Voting Deadlocks \& Conflicting Preferences:} When members have opposing tastes (e.g., budget vs. luxury, Japanese cuisine vs. BBQ), tied votes cause inertia and frequently result in canceled plans.
    \item \textbf{Post-Event Expense Splitting Friction:} Tracking who paid for specific stops and manually calculating uneven debts creates social awkwardness and accounting errors.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Multi-EventType Support: Beyond Just Vacations}
Rather than focusing exclusively on multi-day travel, TripMate AI optimizes search heuristics and UI cards for six distinct daily group scenarios:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{1. Dining (\texttt{DINING}):} Curates restaurants, extracts signature dishes/menus, checks dietary restrictions, and provides price per person.
    \item \textbf{2. Cafe \& Catch-ups (\texttt{HANGOUT}):} Finds coffee shops filtered by vibe (quiet for work, aesthetic photo spots, rooftop lounges).
    \item \textbf{3. Entertainment (\texttt{ENTERTAINMENT}):} Suggests recreational spots (karaoke, bowling, escape rooms, arcade) with ticket costs and capacity limits.
    \item \textbf{4. Sightseeing (\texttt{SIGHTSEEING}):} Identifies cultural landmarks, museums, and exhibitions with operating hours and parking tips.
    \item \textbf{5. Multi-Day Travel (\texttt{TRAVEL}):} Sequences multi-day itineraries, optimizes travel routes, and compares accommodations.
    \item \textbf{6. Custom Events (\texttt{CUSTOM}):} Provides open-ended collaborative planning for birthdays, picnics, or team-building events.
\end{itemize}

\section{Key Technical Innovations}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{AI Conflict Resolver Agent:} When group votes are split (e.g., 3 votes for Japanese vs. 3 votes for BBQ), the agent analyzes members' comments and budget criteria to generate an optimal compromise (e.g., a venue serving both styles or re-sequencing the stops across dinner and late-night supper).
    \item \textbf{Real-Time Interactive AI Chat:} Members can brainstorm directly with the AI assistant via WebSocket/SSE streaming, instantly converting suggested locations into draft timeline cards.
    \item \textbf{Optimal 3-Table Debt Settlement Engine:} Eliminates messy calculations with a mathematically minimal 3-table schema (\texttt{expenses}, \texttt{expense\_splits}, \texttt{settlements}). Net individual balance is computed directly:
    \begin{equation}
        \text{Net Balance}_u = \sum \text{Paid Amount}_u - \sum \text{Owed Share}_u
    \end{equation}
    An automated greedy matching algorithm then calculates the minimum peer-to-peer transfers required to clear all balances.
    \item \textbf{High-Performance Integration Layer:} Redis caching layer guarantees sub-200ms location lookups and shields external APIs from quota limits.
\end{itemize}
