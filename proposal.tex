\section{Introduction \& Project Idea}

\subsection{The Problem}
Organizing activities for a group of 3--10+ people (a trip, a dinner, a weekend hangout) is painful in practice:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Manual research} across maps, booking sites and reviews takes days.
\item \textbf{Schedule overlap} across members with different free windows ($\pm\delta$ tolerance) is a combinatorial puzzle.
\item \textbf{Conflicting preferences} (beach vs. mountain, budget vs. comfort) lead to decision paralysis.
\item \textbf{Fragmented chat} on Zalo/Messenger buries plans and links.
\item \textbf{One organizer} carries the entire load and gets blamed if the group is unhappy.
\item \textbf{Expense splitting} after the trip is tedious and awkward.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Solution}
\textbf{TripMate AI} turns group planning into a guided, consensus-driven flow in under 15 minutes: members chat freely in a shared room $\rightarrow$ an AI agent asks clarifying questions $\rightarrow$ the agent researches and proposes an itinerary $\rightarrow$ the group votes leg-by-leg $\rightarrow$ the plan is locked and expenses are auto-settled.

\section{Platform Features}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Group Room:} Shared chat room, invite by link. Members chat freely --- budget, dietary needs, dates and preferences are shared naturally in conversation or collected by the agent when it detects they are needed. 6 event types: \texttt{TRAVEL}, \texttt{DINING}, \texttt{HANGOUT}, \texttt{ENTERTAINMENT}, \texttt{SIGHTSEEING}, \texttt{CUSTOM}. Roles: Host, Member, Viewer.
\item \textbf{AI Planning Hub:} One-click itinerary generation, live flight/hotel/ticket lookup, dietary-aware dining suggestions, visa/passport checks.
\item \textbf{Voting \& Customization:} Leg-by-leg UP/DOWN voting with instant alternatives, live AI chat to tweak the plan, manual drag-and-drop editor.
\item \textbf{Map \& Utilities:} Interactive map (Mapbox), weather-based packing checklist, PDF export, Vietnamese/English UI.
\item \textbf{Expense Splitting:} Tracks advances and payments, supports equal/exact/percentage splits, computes minimum peer-to-peer settlements.
\end{itemize}

% 3. THE AI AGENT

\section{The AI Agent: Tools, Skills \& Operational Flow}

\subsection{Model Layer}
The agent is powered by \textbf{DeepSeek-V4}, utilizing two operational tiers within a unified context:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Flash} --- fast chat interaction, room message parsing, and tool-call formatting.
\item \textbf{Pro} --- deep reasoning for constraint solving, spatial planning, and preference arbitration.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Tools (External Capabilities)}
Tools are typed callable functions providing real-time data:
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l X l@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Tool} & \textbf{Function \& Parameters $\rightarrow$ Output} & \textbf{Provider} \\
\midrule
\texttt{search\_places} & query, location, radius $\rightarrow$ POIs, ratings, opening hours & Google Places \\
\texttt{search\_flights} & origin, destination, dates, pax $\rightarrow$ flight schedules \& fares & Amadeus \\
\texttt{search\_hotels} & location, dates, guests $\rightarrow$ lodging options \& rates & Booking.com \\
\texttt{search\_activities} & location, category $\rightarrow$ tickets, tours \& activities & Klook \\
\texttt{check\_visa} & nationality, destination $\rightarrow$ visa \& passport requirements & Passport Index \\
\texttt{get\_weather} & location, date range $\rightarrow$ forecast \& weather alerts & OpenWeatherMap \\
\texttt{get\_route} & origin, destination, mode, departure\_time $\rightarrow$ duration, distance & Mapbox \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
\textbf{Execution policy:} Independent tool calls (e.g., weather + flights + hotels) run \textbf{in parallel}; dependent calls (e.g., routing between chosen POIs) execute sequentially. All calls are logged in \texttt{agent\_logs}.

\subsection{Agent Skills}
Internal capabilities governing agent behavior beyond raw API querying:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Research skill:} contextual query formulation, multi-source retrieval, and candidate ranking (Section~\ref{sec:research}).
\item \textbf{Clarification skill:} extracts stated preferences from chat; only queries missing critical parameters without repetitive intake forms.
\item \textbf{Conflict-mediation skill:} negotiates compromises for conflicting preferences (e.g., beach vs. mountain) through hybrid options.
\item \textbf{Local-repair skill:} patches downvoted itinerary legs with pre-scored alternatives without full re-planning.
\item \textbf{Validation skill:} verifies feasibility (prices $> 0$, dates within window, valid opening hours) before presenting options.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Research Skill: Finding Places, Hotels \& Restaurants}
\label{sec:research}
The Research skill transforms group preferences into vetted, ranked candidates through a streamlined process:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Contextual Querying:} Embeds dietary needs, group size, and location proximity into queries (e.g., \texttt{search\_places("halal seafood restaurant", near=Hotel, party=6)}).
  \item \textbf{Multi-Source Retrieval:} Queries relevant APIs (Google Places for POIs/dining, Booking.com for hotels, Klook for activities).
  \item \textbf{Hard Filtering:} Eliminates candidates exceeding group budget cap, violating dietary rules, lacking capacity, or closed during visit slots.
  \item \textbf{Heuristic Scoring:} Ranks viable options via a weighted utility score:
  \[
  \text{Score}(c) = w_1 \cdot \text{Rating} + w_2 \cdot \text{PriceFit} + w_3 \cdot \text{Proximity} + w_4 \cdot \text{PreferenceMatch}
  \]
  where weights $w_i$ adapt to chat cues (e.g., higher $w_2$ for budget constraints; negative penalties for previously rejected attributes).
  \item \textbf{Selection \& Standbys:} The top candidate forms the proposed leg, while 2--3 next-best options are retained as instant voting alternatives.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Destination Discovery: Unset Destinations}
\label{sec:discovery}
When the destination is unspecified (e.g., \textit{``warm place near Hanoi, 3 days, hike + beach, \$150/pax''}):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Stage A (Candidate Generation):} LLM analyzes constraints (duration $\leq$ 3 days $\rightarrow$ travel $\leq$ 2h; budget \$150 $\rightarrow$ domestic; hike + beach $\rightarrow$ Cat Ba / Ha Long shortlist).
  \item \textbf{Stage B (Live Validation):} Parallel tool checks verify weather (\texttt{get\_weather}), flight/travel costs (\texttt{search\_flights}), and attraction accessibility. Surviving top 2--3 destinations are presented to the group for a preliminary consensus vote before detailed itinerary planning.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Place Selection Reasoning: Spatial \& Temporal Coherence}
\label{sec:place_select}
Individual scores do not guarantee a smooth schedule. The agent selects places based on geographic feasibility, realistic travel times, and logical flow:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Geographic Clustering:} Groups candidate POIs into spatial clusters relative to an anchor (e.g., hotel). The agent favors cluster-coherent choices (e.g., picking a restaurant 400\,m from the morning attraction over a slightly higher-rated one 14\,km away to avoid wasted transit).
  \item \textbf{Half-Day Time Blocks:} Partitions days into Morning, Afternoon, and Evening blocks. Stops within a block are restricted to the same geographic cluster.
  \item \textbf{Anti-Zigzag Optimization:} Analyzes route geometry and applies nearest-neighbor sequencing to flexible stops, preventing backtracking across the city.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Spatial Planning: Routing, Distance, Time \& Transport}
\label{sec:spatial}
Spatial planning validates that the itinerary is physically feasible within group time budgets:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Realistic Transit \& Timing:} Computes real road distance and travel time via \texttt{get\_route} with departure timestamps. Peak-hour transitions (7--9 am, 5--7 pm) automatically include a 30--50\% traffic buffer.
  \item \textbf{Feasibility Checks:} Enforces $\text{Arrival} + \text{Duration} \leq \text{Closing Time}$ with $\ge 15$\,min connection slack. Preserves fixed meal slots (lunch 11:30--13:30, dinner 18:00--20:30) and anchors timed-entry attractions.
  \item \textbf{Transport Mode Selection:} Chooses optimal transit based on group size, distance, and context:
\end{enumerate}

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}l l X@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Distance / Context} & \textbf{Default Mode} & \textbf{Rationale} \\
\midrule
$< 1.5$ km & Walking & Zero cost, walkable in dense areas, no waiting time. \\
2--15 km (flexible / youth) & Motorbike / Scooter & Highly agile in traffic, easy parking, cost-effective, scenic exploration. \\
2--10 km (groups / rain) & Grab / Taxi & Weather-safe, convenient point-to-point transit for small groups. \\
10--40 km ($\ge 5$ pax) & Private Van / Car & Keeps group together, lower cost than multi-taxi convoy. \\
$> 40$ km & Bus / Flight & Optimizes transit duration versus budget limits. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
\textit{Override:} Modes dynamically adjust if the group specifies a preference (e.g., \textit{``rent motorbikes''}) or during adverse weather.

\textbf{Example Spatial Optimization (Da Nang, 6 pax):} Rather than scheduling distant attractions on the same morning, the agent anchors Bà Nà Hills (35\,km) for the morning with on-site lunch (saving a 70\,km round-trip), returns off-peak at 14:00 by private van, and assigns the nearby beach for sunset. Total daily transit is reduced from 180\,min to 90\,min.

\subsection{Agentic Reasoning Loop}
TripMate AI runs an adaptive \textbf{Think $\rightarrow$ Act $\rightarrow$ Observe} loop rather than a rigid pipeline:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Think:} Evaluates the structured session state (Section 3.9) to choose the single next best action (call a tool, query the group, or emit proposal) using DeepSeek-Pro.
\item \textbf{Act:} Executes the selected action (e.g., invoke \texttt{search\_hotels} or Clarification skill).
\item \textbf{Observe:} Parses output, updates state, and evaluates stop conditions.
\item \textbf{Stop Condition:} Terminates when the itinerary meets all constraints, consensus is reached, or round limits (Section 3.10) are met.
\end{enumerate}
The 5 UX stages in Figure~\ref{fig:lifecycle} provide user-facing checkpoints, while internal execution remains dynamic and dependency-driven.

\subsection{Context Management}
\begin{itemize}
\item Maintains a running \textbf{structured JSON state} (extracted constraints, budget, booked legs) updated each turn, avoiding bloated token re-transmission.
\item The itinerary is maintained as a stateful object of uniquely ID'd legs, enabling atomic local repairs.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Retry \& Error Handling}
\begin{itemize}
\item External API calls allow up to \textbf{2 retries} with exponential backoff before falling back to cached data (Redis, TTL 6--24h) with an ``outdated'' indicator.
\item Unresolvable tool failures are stated transparently to avoid hallucinations.
\item Clarification interactions are capped at \textbf{3 rounds}; unaddressed inputs default to sensible heuristics.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Learning Feedback Loop}
\begin{itemize}
\item Rejection reasons (price, distance, style) are logged in the session state.
\item Subsequent candidate proposals filter out attributes matching rejected patterns.
\item Feedback is strictly session-scoped, resetting for subsequent trips.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Worked Examples}
\textbf{Scenario A --- Known Destination (Da Nang, 3 days, 6 pax):}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{Turn 1 (Input):} Dates/destination known; diet (halal) parsed from chat $\rightarrow$ Agent asks only for missing budget.
\item \textit{Turn 2 (Research):} Parallel lookup (\texttt{search\_hotels}, \texttt{search\_places}, \texttt{get\_weather}) $\rightarrow$ Filters budget/capacity, selects top hotel + 2 backups.
\item \textit{Turn 3 (Spatial Planning):} Clusters POIs $\rightarrow$ Anchors Bà Nà Hills (35\,km) to Day 2 morning, separates Marble Mountains to Day 3 to prevent zigzagging.
\item \textit{Turn 4 (Routing):} \texttt{get\_route} assigns Grab van (6 pax), applies peak-hour buffers $\rightarrow$ Validates opening hours $\rightarrow$ Generates proposal cards.
\item \textit{Turn 5 (Repair):} Member downvotes lunch spot $\rightarrow$ Local-repair immediately injects pre-scored halal alternative.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Scenario B --- Unset Destination (``Warm, near Hanoi, 3 days, hike + beach''):}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textit{Turn 1 (Discovery):} LLM generates candidate shortlist (Cat Ba, Ha Long, Ninh Binh) balancing hike/beach criteria.
\item \textit{Turn 2 (Validation):} Checks weather and travel costs in parallel $\rightarrow$ Drops high-rain candidate $\rightarrow$ Presents top 2 to group vote.
\item \textit{Turn 3 onwards:} Group selects Cat Ba $\rightarrow$ Resumes standard itinerary research and spatial planning.
\end{itemize}

% 4. BACKEND ARCHITECTURE
% ==========================================
\section{Backend Architecture}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Runtime:} Python 3.11+, FastAPI (async, auto OpenAPI docs).
\item \textbf{Database:} PostgreSQL + SQLAlchemy 2.0 (async) + Alembic migrations.
\item \textbf{Cache/Broker:} Redis --- caches tool results, rate-limits, WebSocket pub/sub.
\item \textbf{Background jobs:} Celery / BackgroundTasks for PDF export (WeasyPrint) and email.
\item \textbf{Settlement engine:} 3 tables (\texttt{expenses}, \texttt{expense\_splits}, \texttt{settlements}); greedy matching on net balances $\text{Net}_u = \sum \text{Paid}_u - \sum \text{Owed}_u$ minimizes transfer count.
\end{itemize}

% 6. DEPLOYMENT & SECURITY
% ==========================================
\section{Deployment \& Security}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Pipeline:} Docker Compose (local/staging), Vercel (frontend CDN), Render/VPS (backend), GitHub Actions (CI).
\item \textbf{Security:} bcrypt password hashing, JWT access + Redis-rotated refresh tokens, RBAC, \texttt{bleach} sanitization, Pydantic v2 schema validation on all AI output.
\item \textbf{Data privacy:} all trip-specific information (dietary needs, budget, passport details) lives only in the session chat and the agent's session state; it is not stored in permanent user profiles. Access is scoped by RBAC and not shared outside the room.
\item \textbf{Rate limiting \& audit:} Redis-backed limits on external and AI endpoints; all tool calls and token usage logged in \texttt{agent\_logs}.
\end{itemize}
